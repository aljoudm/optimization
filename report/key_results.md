# Key results (reference draft for Section 3 and the paper)

Every number is followed by the file it comes from (folder `results/` unless stated). The numbers are regenerated by `scripts/run_all_cases.m` and `scripts/make_stage5.m`.
**C_total does not include the cabins: C_shelter = 0 is a PLACEHOLDER (no price found), so the real additional cost will be higher.**
Base values: P_d,DAS = 0.969 (`zeng2024`), P_c,DAS = 0.983 (`wang2024`), P_c,drone = 0.89 (`khan_uav_thermal`, proxy), T_resp = 300 s (PLACEHOLDER), T_launch = 10 s (`advexure_dock3`, VERIFY),
A_ver,min = 0.9 (PLACEHOLDER), R_resp = 4,350 m, scenarios 3 / 6 / 12 drones.

## 4. Verification of Hot cells: optimal placement versus no optimization (T_resp = 300 s, P_d,DAS = 0.969)

| Scenario (drones) | Optimal | Evenly spread on the boundary (pairs) | 2b: evenly over the same candidate stations | 2c: best phase of the even boundary layout | One drone per station, even |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| low (3) | 9 / 36 (25 %) | 0 | 0 | 9 (25 %) | 0 |
| medium (6) | 22 / 36 (61 %) | 7 (19 %) | 2 (6 %) | 16 (44 %) | 0 |
| high (12) | 34 / 36 (94 %) | 12 (33 %) | 22 (61 %) | 25 (69 %) | 0 |

Source: `baseline2_even_drones.csv`, maps `map_*.pdf`, `map_baseline2_*.pdf`, `map_baseline2b_*.pdf`.
Drones used by the optimum: 3 drones at 2 stations (low), 6 at 3 (medium), 12 at 6 (high) (`summary_runs.csv`).
A single drone per station has a ready probability of 0.635 < A_ver,min = 0.9, so it never verifies a Hot cell on its own.
Remark for the paper: the strongest regular layout (2c) is still below the optimum for medium (16 vs 22) and high (25 vs 34); for the low scenario the best phase reaches the optimum (9).
